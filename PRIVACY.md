# Privacy Policy

Naru is a client for macOS Screen Sharing on iPad, Android, and Windows. This policy covers all three apps.

Effective October 14, 2026.

## Information we collect

The developer collects no personal information.

- Naru has no accounts, and the developer runs no server.
- The apps contain no analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

The apps keep the following on your device only, and never send it to the developer:

- The name, address, user name, and last connection time of each Mac you save
- Passwords you choose to save: in the keychain on iPad, encrypted with an Android Keystore key on Android, and in
  Windows Credential Manager for this PC only on Windows
- The public key of each Mac you connect to, used to confirm it is the same Mac next time
- App settings
- The app's log, which the system keeps for the current launch

Depending on your system settings, everything except passwords may be included in iCloud or Google backups and in
transfers to a new device. Passwords never leave the device.

## Network connections

- The apps connect only to the Macs you choose. Screen, audio, keyboard, and pointer data travel directly between your
  device and your Mac, never through the developer or any third party.
- The apps use local network access to find Macs with Screen Sharing turned on.
- With the shared clipboard on, clipboard text is exchanged between your device and your Mac during a session. You can
  turn it off in the app.

## Diagnostics

Help > Export Diagnostics on iPad, or Settings > Help > Export Diagnostics on Android and Windows, saves the app's log
as a text file and opens the share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it. The log records what
the app did, such as connection stages, errors, and the input language; it never contains passwords, keys, typed
text, or clipboard contents.

## Sharing with third parties

The developer collects no personal information and shares none. The store you got the app from (App Store, Google
Play, Microsoft Store) or TestFlight may collect crash reports with your consent, under that store's privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

Removing a Mac in the app deletes its details, its saved password, and its public key when no other saved Mac uses the
same address. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.
